UrbanX Website – My Honest Shopping Experience & Review

By Gayatri Ghadge

Introduction

Hi everyone! I'm Gayatri Ghadge, and today I'm going to share my honest shopping experience with UrbanX.

Recently, I came across the UrbanX website through my Instagram algorithm. The website has been trending quite a lot these days, and I really liked the way their products looked. One particular pair of trousers caught my attention, and without giving it much thought, I decided to place an order.

My Order Details

Website: UrbanX

Product: Women's Linen-Style Wide-Leg Trouser

Price: ₹599

Size: M (28-inch waist as listed)

Order Date: 7th November 2026

Delivery Date: 11th November 2026

Delivery Partner: Blue Dart

1. Delivery Experience

I placed my order on 7th November 2026, and it was delivered on 11th November 2026 through Blue Dart. Talking about the delivery experience, I would say it was fairly decent. The delivery took around four days, so you can expect your order to arrive within approximately 3–5 days.

2. Packaging

Let's start with the packaging. The packaging was decent and satisfactory. It wasn't anything fancy or premium, but it was okay. I didn't have any major complaints about it.

3. Product Quality

Now, coming to the most important part – the product itself!

The trousers were advertised as linen-style, wide-leg pants with a full-length design. However, when I received them, the material didn't quite meet my expectations. The fabric was very see-through, and honestly, the overall material quality didn't feel that great to me.

There was quite a noticeable difference between what I expected from the product pictures and what I actually received.

4. Size and Fitting

Next comes the fitting. I ordered an M size, which was mentioned as a 28-inch waist. The waist measurement seemed okay, but unfortunately, the trousers didn't fit me properly around the thigh area.

Since my waist is comparatively smaller and my thighs are broader, the overall fit wasn't comfortable for me. So, despite liking the design online, I wasn't happy with how the trousers actually fitted.

5. Customer Support

This brings me to the most disappointing part of my entire shopping experience – customer support.

Since the trousers didn't fit me properly, I wanted to contact customer care to understand the return or exchange process. But here's where things became frustrating. I couldn't find any proper customer care service or direct support option. The only way to reach out to them seemed to be through email.

They had also provided a contact number, so I tried calling it. However, the number was switched off and wasn't working. I couldn't get any immediate assistance or speak to someone who could help me with my concern.

Honestly, customer support was a major disappointment. When a customer spends money on a product and faces an issue, having an accessible support team is the least they can expect from an online shopping website.

6. My Overall Experience

Overall, this purchase turned out to be a disappointing experience for me. I spent ₹599, which is almost ₹600, but unfortunately, I wasn't satisfied with the product quality or fitting.

The delivery was fine and the packaging was decent, but the lack of proper customer support made the experience even worse.

I genuinely wanted to contact their customer care team and find a solution, whether through a return or an exchange, but I couldn't get through to anyone.

7. My Suggestion to Buyers

So, this was my honest shopping experience with UrbanX. I'm not saying everyone will have the same experience, but I'm sharing what happened with my order so that you can consider these points before making a purchase.

Before ordering from UrbanX, make sure you carefully check the product details, size chart, fabric information, and return or exchange policy.

Conclusion

Thank you for reading my review. I hope my experience helps you make a more informed shopping decision!

I've also attached all the pictures I could, so you can have a closer look at the product and get a better understanding of my experience. I believe pictures can sometimes tell you more than words, so feel free to check them out and form your own opinion.

Happy reading, and most importantly, shop wisely!
